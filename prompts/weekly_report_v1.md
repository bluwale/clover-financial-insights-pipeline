# Weekly executive report

Give the owner a week-in-review. Using only values present in the JSON, cover:

- **Revenue trends** — net/gross, order count, AOV, and week-over-week growth (`wow_growth_pct`).
- **Product & size performance** — the `top_category`, notable sell-through and demand-share signals
  from `size_curve`, and new-arrival sell-through (`new_arrival_sell_through`) where present.
- **Inventory warnings** — stock-out risks (`stock_risks`) and dead-stock / slow SKUs (`slow_skus`)
  worth reordering or clearing.
- **Recommendations** — 2–4 concrete actions, each tied to a specific number, SKU, or category from
  the data.
