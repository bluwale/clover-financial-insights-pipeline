# Monthly business intelligence digest

Give the owner a month-level readout. This is the fullest report; still keep each section tight.
Using only values present in the JSON, cover:

- **Revenue** — net/gross, order count, AOV, month-over-month growth (`mom_growth_pct`), and the
  payment-method split.
- **Category & size mix** — `top_category` and the `size_curve` sell-through / reorder-weight
  signals; call out sizes or categories selling through vs accumulating.
- **New-arrival sell-through** — how recent stock is moving (`new_arrival_sell_through`) and any
  `underperforming_arrivals`.
- **Inventory health** — dead-stock aging, stock-out risks, and slow SKUs.
- **Recommendations** — 3–5 concrete, data-backed actions for the coming month.
